# Standalone ML Paper Blueprint

Use this after inspecting all research materials when the user requests a full
blueprint or the workspace is too complex for the compact default. Generate
competing stories only when the material supports genuine alternatives, then
fill the selected paper program and missing-evidence plan. Empty fields are
research decisions, not invitations to invent content.

## Contents

- 0–4: inventory, candidate stories, contract, promise stack, and proof modules
- 5–8: reader-belief graph, claim/evidence records, and missing evidence
- 9–15: identity anchor, artifact decisions/cards, formal/case modules, and routing
- 16–19: section dependencies, result arguments, abstract, and introduction
- 20–22: reviewer red team, memory tests, and page budget
